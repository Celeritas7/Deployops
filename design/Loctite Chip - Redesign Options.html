My pick for a shop-floor QC tool: C or D. Both put dark text on a light backing, which is the only combo that holds full contrast over both white paper and black drawing lines, and they degrade most gracefully in grayscale print. A is the smallest change from today if you want to keep the dark identity. B maximises see-through but the multi-strip look is busier up close.

To wire in: these all target your existing .lt-callout / .lt-grade / .lt-torque structure — swap the rule block for the chosen option. Note your real chip also has the drag .lt-grip + selected-state tools; those still work, but for the lighter look set .lt-callout{overflow:visible} and drop the box-shadow/opaque .lt-grip background to match. Happy to apply the winner directly to index.html.
